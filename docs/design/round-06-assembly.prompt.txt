Use case: ui-mockup. A highly authored modern Chinese people-and-organizations collaboration application called 康米巨星. Flat modern-leftist constructivist style reinterpreted with visual wit and calm functional use, not retro propaganda. Wide desktop layout: off-white only barely neutral background, white spacious working surfaces, black strong typography, red slender signals. Compact black topbar with custom small geometric brand, horizontal nav 人物 组织 未读. Left person list using widely spaced editorial rows and distinctive square illustrative avatars. Right detail panel is an asymmetric assembly: name 林川 in 44px modern Chinese sans and red outlined status badge 人事审核; below, work assignee chips with a small angled flag/diamond badge 人事负责, clearly separate from state. Main record text 19px, metadata 14px. Minimal composer a red circular cross aligned with a wide open writing line. Three-dimensional NOTHING: no shadows, no glass, no texture. Unexpected graphic signature: stepped corner cuts and a single dashed architectural line connecting header to timeline, very restrained. Use generous whitespace without shrinking content. Default person avatar playful geometric masked observer; organization avatar a tiny absurd communal building with windows for eyes. No first-letter avatars, alias or intro. No slogans, charts or fake statistics. Design should feel memorable and mature, radically beyond a conventional red-black SaaS dashboard.
